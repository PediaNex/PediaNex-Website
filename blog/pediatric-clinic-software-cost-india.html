How Much Does Pediatric Clinic Software Cost in India?

Published July 25, 2026 · PediaNex Team

Most cloud-based pediatric clinic software in India is priced per doctor per month as a subscription, rather than a large upfront license fee — which keeps the initial cost manageable for a solo or small practice. What varies most between vendors is what's included in the base price: patient records and scheduling are usually standard, while pediatric-specific features like vaccination tracking, growth charting, and WhatsApp prescriptions may be included or sold separately. "How much does this cost" is usually the second question a pediatrician asks, right after "does it actually help." The honest answer depends heavily on the pricing model and what's bundled in — here's how to read a vendor's pricing page, and the costs that don't show up on one at all.

The three pricing models you'll actually see

Per-doctor, per-month subscription

The most common model for cloud-based clinic software today. Cost scales with the number of doctors using the system, with no large upfront payment — a solo pediatrician pays for one seat, a multi-doctor clinic pays per doctor added.

Flat clinic-wide subscription

Some vendors charge a single monthly or annual fee regardless of doctor count, which can work out cheaper for a clinic with several doctors but is worth checking against actual usage if the clinic is solo or has just one or two doctors.

One-time license, self-hosted

Older, less common model: a larger upfront payment for software installed and run on the clinic's own systems, with no ongoing subscription but typically separate costs for updates, support, and hosting infrastructure.

What to actually check on a pricing page

  • What's bundled vs. add-on: vaccination tracking, growth charts, and WhatsApp prescriptions can be core features on one platform and paid extras on another — the headline price alone doesn't tell you this.
  • Per-doctor vs. per-clinic: a lower headline price on a per-doctor plan can cost more than a flat plan once a clinic has three or four doctors.
  • Free tier limits: a "free forever" plan usually caps patient count, doctor seats, or feature access — worth checking those numbers against your actual patient volume before investing time in data entry.
  • Support and training: whether onboarding, data migration help, and ongoing support are included, or billed separately.
In short: the headline monthly number matters less than what's actually included at that price. Two platforms at similar per-doctor pricing can differ significantly in what a pediatric clinic gets out of the box.

The cost of not switching

Staying on paper or a spreadsheet has a cost too — it's just distributed differently. It shows up as staff time spent on manual lookups and entry, vaccination doses that go unflagged until a parent happens to ask, and the growing cost of migrating years of paper history if the clinic eventually does switch. None of that appears on an invoice, which is exactly why it's easy to underweight when comparing "free" (paper) against a monthly subscription.

Frequently asked questions

How is pediatric clinic software usually priced in India?

Most cloud-based pediatric clinic software is priced per doctor, per month, as a subscription rather than a large upfront license fee. Some vendors offer flat clinic-wide pricing instead, and a few still sell traditional one-time-license, self-hosted software.

What's usually included in the base price?

Typically patient records, appointment scheduling, and prescription generation are included in a base plan. Pediatric-specific features like vaccination tracking and growth charting, WhatsApp integration, or a parent portal may be included or offered as add-ons depending on the vendor.

Is free clinic software actually free?

Free tiers usually cap patient volume, doctor count, or feature access, and are often a funnel toward a paid plan once the clinic grows. It's worth checking those limits against your actual patient volume before committing time to data entry on a free tier.

Is staying on paper or Excel actually free?

Not in practice. The ongoing cost shows up as staff time spent on manual entry and lookup, missed or delayed vaccination doses that go unflagged, and the eventual cost of migrating years of paper records if the clinic switches to software later.
