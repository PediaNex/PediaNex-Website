Best Pediatric EMR Software in India (2026 Buyer's Guide)

Published September 17, 2026 · PediaNex Team

Short answer: The best pediatric EMR software in India is one built specifically for children: it plots WHO and IAP growth charts automatically, tracks every dose of the IAP immunization schedule with due and overdue alerts, sends prescriptions to parents on WhatsApp, and gives parents record access without an app. Generic EMRs adapted from adult medicine usually miss at least two of these. For an independent clinic, expect to pay roughly ₹400–₹1,000 per doctor per month for a cloud product that needs no server.

Most EMR comparison lists rank software by feature count. For a pediatric practice that is the wrong measure: a product with 200 features and no percentile growth chart still sends your staff back to paper for the one thing you do at every well-child visit. This guide is the checklist we'd use if we were buying.

The five things a pediatric EMR must do

1. Growth charts that plot themselves

Enter weight, height and head circumference once and the chart should appear, with percentiles and z-scores against WHO standards under 5 and IAP charts from 5 to 18. If a vendor shows you a table of numbers and calls it a growth chart, keep looking.

2. The full IAP vaccination schedule, per dose

Each child should carry a live schedule from birth dose to the adolescent Tdap and HPV doses, with due, overdue and given status and the brand and batch recorded. Catch-up handling for late starters matters more than the happy path.

3. Prescriptions that reach the parent's phone

Weight-based dosing, a saved favourites list, and a PDF prescription delivered on WhatsApp. Parents lose paper; they don't lose WhatsApp chats.

4. Parent access without an app download

Asking parents to install an app has low uptake. A browser portal using the child's patient ID and date of birth gets used.

5. No server, no IT person

Independent clinics should not be buying hardware. Cloud software that works in a browser, caches recent records for patchy internet, and installs as an app on a phone is the right fit.

Questions to ask on every demo

  1. Show me a 9-month-old's growth chart after one visit. Watch how many clicks it takes.
  2. Show me a child who missed their 6-week vaccines and came at 5 months. This tests catch-up logic.
  3. How do I export all my data if I leave? A good answer is a file format, not a phone number.
  4. What happens when the internet drops mid-OPD?
  5. What is the total yearly price for my number of doctors, including setup and data migration?
Red flags: per-patient pricing that punishes busy clinics, annual contracts with no trial, "growth chart module" sold as an add-on, and no way to export records.
