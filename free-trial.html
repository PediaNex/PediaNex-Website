The free two months
Is there really no card required?

Correct. We don't take payment details to start, so nothing can auto-charge you. At the end of the two months we ask you what you'd like to do.

Is it a limited version during the trial?

No. It's the complete product, for every doctor in your clinic, for the full two months.

When do the two months start?

The day your clinic is set up and ready to see patients on it — not the day you first get in touch.

Security
How is patient data protected?

Every clinical page sits behind a signed session, and every record lives in access-controlled cloud storage. We're actively tightening this further as we move from a single-clinic tool to a multi-practice product.

Using it day to day
Do I need special hardware?

No. PediaNex runs in any browser and installs as an app on phone, tablet, or desktop.

What if my clinic's internet is unreliable?

Recent charts and patient lookups are cached on the device, so you can keep working through a short outage. Everything syncs once you're back online.

Do parents need to download an app?

No. The parent portal works in any mobile browser.

Onboarding
Can you migrate our existing patient records?

Yes, and it's free. This is exactly how our own historical records got into the system in the first place, so it's a proven path, not a first attempt.

What if we're still on paper?

Then there's nothing to migrate and nothing to type in. Records build as you see patients, and parents enter their own details by scanning a QR code at the desk.

How long does setup take?

It depends on how much existing data needs migrating. We'll give you a real estimate on the walkthrough call, not a number picked to sound good on a landing page.
